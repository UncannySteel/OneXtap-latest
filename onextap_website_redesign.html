FAQ

Questions,
answered.

How is my data stored and protected? +
Your data is stored locally on your device by default. Premium users get optional encrypted cloud backup using AES-256 encryption. We never sell or share your personal information with third parties.
Do I have control over AI suggestions? +
Completely. AI-generated answers are shown to you for review before anything is submitted. You can edit, reject, or accept each suggestion individually.
What happens when I run out of free AI credits? +
The autofill feature continues to work without limits — only the AI-powered answer personalization requires credits. Upgrade to Premium for unlimited AI usage.
Can I use different profiles for different job types? +
Yes. Create separate profiles for different roles, industries, or seniority levels. Switch between them with one click before filling any application.
Is Onextap an Applicant Tracking System? +
No. Onextap is a personal productivity tool focused entirely on simplifying the application process. We don't connect to employers or influence hiring decisions.
