Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 2026

1

Data Ownership & Local-First Philosophy

Onextap is built on the principle of Data Sovereignty. By default, all personal information, resume data, and application history are stored locally on your device within the browser's secure storage.

2

Data Collection and Usage

Personal Information: We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. Data is used solely to facilitate the autofill functionality of job applications.

AI Processing: When you use the "AI Refine" feature, the job description and your selected saved answer are sent to our secure AI partners (Anthropic/Google). This data is processed in a "stateless" manner and is not used to train global AI models.

Cloud Sync (Optional): If you enable Cloud Backup, your data is transmitted via SSL/TLS encryption to our secure database (Supabase) to allow syncing across your devices.

3

Third-Party Services

Onextap interacts with the following services:

  • Supabase: For encrypted authentication and cloud storage.
  • Anthropic/Google: For natural language processing and resume parsing.
  • Dodo Payments: For secure payment processing (we never see or store your credit card details).
4

Security

We employ industry-standard encryption. Your "Master Profile" is yours alone. We do not have "backdoor" access to your locally stored data.

Questions?

If you have any concerns about your privacy, we're happy to help.
