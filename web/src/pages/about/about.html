01Company — About

Not an ATS.

the other side of the form.

Applicant tracking systems were built for the people reading applications. Onextap is built for the person writing them: a Chrome extension that fills the application for you — every field, on every job board — and drafts the written answers in your own words.

01 / The problem

The same eleven questions, forever.

Greenhouse names the field one way. Workday names it another. Lever buries it on page three. The answers never change — only the labels, the order and the number of clicks.

02 / What it does

Learn the form once. Fill it everywhere.

Onextap recognises the fields on an application, fills them from your profile, and drafts the written answers from the posting in front of you and the answers you have already given. A field it has not met before is flagged, never guessed — map it once and it fills itself on every board after that.

03 / What it isn't

A tool for the recruiter.

  • ◆ No pipelines, no scoring, no ranking of candidates.
  • ◆ Every answer stays editable before it is saved or submitted. Nothing goes out unread.
  • ◆ Your profile stays in your browser — here is exactly what leaves it.

Chrome, Edge, Brave and Opera.

On Greenhouse, Lever, Workday, Ashby, iCIMS, Workable, SmartRecruiters and Taleo. Free forever, no card — three AI credits to start, then $5 a month if you want unlimited drafts.
