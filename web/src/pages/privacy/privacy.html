03Company — Privacy

Yours, locally.

well… mostly. here's the rest.

The plain version: what Onextap keeps on your device, what leaves it when you ask for something and who handles it, and what your account holds. Written down, not left to inference. Last updated September 2026.

01 / On your device

Your profile lives in your browser.

Your details, saved answers, cover letters and resumes are kept in the browser's own storage — chrome.storage.local in the extension, local storage on the dashboard — and never in our database. The dashboard hands your profile to the extension on your own machine. Autofill uses only that local copy: filling a form sends nothing anywhere. Stored locally is not the same as never sent, though; the next two sections say exactly what goes out, and when.

02 / What leaves it

Only when you ask.

Your profile's text leaves the device only when you use an AI feature, and only the text that feature needs:

  • ◆ Writing an answer or personalising a cover letter sends Groq the question, your draft or template, the job description you pasted or had open, a short summary of your profile (current role, top skills, recent experience) and the saved answers relevant to the question.
  • ◆ Uploading a resume sends the file to Google (Gemini), which reads it into your profile's fields.
  • ◆ Job Matches sends a summary of your profile — skills, job titles, seniority and years of experience, not your full resume — with the listings being ranked, to Gemini, or to Groq when Gemini is unavailable.
  • ◆ Explain my fit sends fuller resume text — your summary and experience — with that one job's description, to Groq.

We do not sell, rent or trade any of it, and we do not use it to train models of our own. What Groq and Google do with text sent through their APIs is governed by their terms, not ours.

03 / What is recorded

Metadata, not your words.

To catch failures, requests to rank job matches and to explain a fit are recorded with Opik, an AI observability service run by Comet. As of this writing those records hold operational metadata only — job counts, search filters, job IDs, success or failure — never the resume, profile or answer text the requests carry. The Onextap team can see them, and uses them only to diagnose and improve the product: never for advertising, and never to build a profile of you. If they are ever to include your text, this page will say so first.

04 / Your account

What the account holds.

Your account, kept by Supabase, holds your email address, display name, credit balance and subscription status — and, if you subscribe, the customer and subscription identifiers Dodo Payments uses for billing. Card details go to Dodo Payments, never to us. Your signed-in session is kept in your browser's local storage, not in a cookie. Everything sent to our servers and to Supabase travels over HTTPS.

05 / Who handles it

The services we use.

  • ◆ Supabase — sign-in, and the account and billing records above.
  • ◆ Groq — writing and improving answers and cover letters, Explain my fit, and the fallback for ranking job matches.
  • ◆ Google (Gemini) — reading uploaded resumes, and the main model for ranking job matches.
  • ◆ Google Sign-In — only if you choose it: your browser goes to Google's own sign-in page, and we receive back your email and name, through Supabase.
  • ◆ Dodo Payments — payments, and the billing portal for invoices and your card.
  • ◆ Opik (Comet) — the operational metadata described in 03.
  • ◆ Resend — delivering feedback-form messages to our team, with a reply-to address if you give one. Nothing is stored.
  • ◆ Google Fonts — this site's typefaces. Loading them, like any request to Google's servers, shows Google your IP address.
  • ◆ Job sources (Adzuna, Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Arbeitnow, Jobicy, Himalayas, Remotive, RemoteOK) — a one-way, read-only feed of public postings. Nothing of yours is sent to them.

06 / The extension

It acts when you tell it to.

The extension asks for a small, specific set of permissions:

  • ◆ storage — to keep your profile on your device.
  • ◆ activeTab and scripting — to read a posting or fill a form on the tab you are looking at, and only after you click something in the extension. It has no standing access to your other tabs, and nothing runs when you simply open a page.
  • ◆ identity — to open Google's own sign-in window when you choose to sign in with Google.

When you ask it to read a posting, it reads the company name and the description text on that page, up to 8,000 characters — not your cookies, not your other tabs, not your browsing history.

07 / Cookies & security

No trackers.

No tracking cookies, and as of this writing no analytics or advertising scripts. If that ever changes, this page will say so first, with a way to opt out. What is on your device is protected by your browser and operating system; we add no separate encryption on top of it, and we have no backdoor into it.

08 / Your say

Keep it, or delete it.

Every drafted answer stays editable before it is saved or submitted: Onextap fills the form; you decide what gets sent. Your profile, resumes and answers exist only on your device, so clearing them in Onextap or uninstalling the extension removes them. Delete account, in the dashboard's settings, cancels any subscription and deletes your account and everything stored with it; until then, we keep account data only while the account exists.
