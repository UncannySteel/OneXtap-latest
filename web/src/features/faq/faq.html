FAQ

Fair questions.

The ones we hear most, before anyone adds it to Chrome.

What does Onextap actually do?

It fills job applications for you — every field, on every job board — and drafts the written answers from the posting in front of you and the answers you've already given. A field it hasn't met before is flagged, never guessed.

Is it really free?

Yes. Autofill is free forever, no card: unlimited applications, multiple profiles, and three AI credits for written answers and cover letters. Premium is $5 a month if you want unlimited drafts.

What does Premium add?

No limits on the AI: unlimited written answers and cover letters, and unlimited fit explanations on your job matches. It's the same AI as Free, without the credit count. Cancel anytime; Premium runs to the end of the month you've paid for. Priority support and early access come with it.

Which job boards does it work on?

Greenhouse, Lever, Workday, Ashby, iCIMS, Workable, SmartRecruiters and Taleo. Another one giving you trouble? Tell us which.

Which browsers?

Chrome, Edge, Brave and Opera.

Where does my data go?

Your profile, answers and cover letters stay in your browser's own storage. What leaves it goes only when you ask: a resume you upload is read by Google's Gemini, a draft is written by Groq from the posting and your profile, and job matching sends a summary of your skills. Your account and billing are kept by Supabase and Dodo Payments. The details.

Will it send anything without me seeing it?

No. Every drafted answer stays editable before it's saved or submitted. Onextap fills the form; you decide what gets sent.

Do I need an account?

A free one. It holds your credits and plan, and the dashboard it opens is where you set up the profile Onextap fills from. The profile itself stays in your browser.
